# What is OpenLake
url: https://openlake.in/philosophy
OpenLake is the open-source society of IIT Bhilai. It builds software the
campus actually uses, teaches students to make their first contribution, and
helps them contribute to open source elsewhere. Its tagline is
"Build. Learn. Contribute."

# Who can join OpenLake
url: https://openlake.in/philosophy
Anyone, from any batch or branch. Activities are public and free to join, and
no prior knowledge is needed to start.

# Do I need experience to join
url: https://openlake.in/philosophy
No. The workshops are aimed at people who have never opened a pull request,
and there are no prerequisites.

# Is there a fee to join
url: https://openlake.in/philosophy
No. There is no fee and no application. Join the Discord, come to a workshop,
or open an issue on any of the repositories.

# How do I join OpenLake
url: https://openlake.in/philosophy
There is no application process. Join the community Discord, attend a
workshop, or open an issue on any OpenLake repository.

# How do I make my first contribution
url: https://openlake.in/philosophy
Four steps: attend a workshop, pick a beginner-friendly issue in an OpenLake
repository and get a maintainer's review on your pull request, join a mentored
programme like FOSS Overflow or DevLabs, then contribute to outside programmes.

# What does OpenLake actually do
url: https://openlake.in/philosophy
Three stages, in order. Build: maintain campus software that has real users
and code review. Learn: free hands-on workshops on Git, Linux, web and app
development. Contribute: help members land contributions in the wider
open-source world.

# Why does OpenLake review pull requests
url: https://openlake.in/philosophy
Because a maintainer's review is the point of the beginner route. OpenLake
also says members should not stay confined to its own repositories, and that
the software should be real and used rather than practice exercises.

# How many projects does OpenLake have

# What campus tools does OpenLake build
url: https://openlake.in/programs
Student_Database_COSA manages student data and Positions of Responsibility.
Campus-Marketplace lets students buy and sell on campus. RateMyCourse collects
course and instructor reviews. Smart-Insti-App gathers institute information
and updates. RideShare handles ridesharing for students and staff.

# What tools are there for coursework
url: https://openlake.in/programs
iitbh-cgpa is a browser extension that calculates CGPA from the AIMS Portal.
iitbh-calendar generates a calendar for IIT Bhilai courses. Homework-Scheduler
helps teachers set deadlines around student commitments. Bhilaipedia is a wiki
for IIT Bhilai.

# What projects are there for electronics students
url: https://openlake.in/programs
bhilaee-labs is a virtual lab platform for Electrical and Electronics
Engineering experiments. bhilaee-simulator is a browser-based circuit
simulator with DC, AC and transient analysis. FOSSI covers free open-source
silicon, EDA tools and chip design.

# What projects involve machine learning
url: https://openlake.in/programs
SmartTriage finds duplicate issues by semantic search and auto-assigns
reviewers from commit history. Photo-Sharing-App groups photos by face.
Smart-Cams adds computer vision to CCTV and mobile cameras. AI-Voice-Agent is
a voice agent that adapts to its user. Speech-Analyser helps improve English
fluency.

# What projects help with placements and careers
url: https://openlake.in/programs
The Centre-for-Career-Planning-and-Services-Portal links students with the
placement cell. career-scraper analyses career trajectories. Knowledge holds
career-planning resources. Interview-Preparation and GSoC_Proposals archive
preparation material and past proposals.

# What is canonforces
url: https://openlake.in/programs
canonforces is a Codeforces companion with a built-in IDE, real-time 1v1 DSA
battles and stat syncing.

# What infrastructure does OpenLake run
url: https://openlake.in/programs
Hub connects OpenLake apps and provides unified APIs. auth-kit provides single
sign-on, JWT access control and role management across the ecosystem. Infra
holds the automation, bots and configuration that keep things running. Bit is
a git server built from scratch for IIT Bhilai.

real-time sports scoring. Find-your-Cluster finds shared interests among peers.
