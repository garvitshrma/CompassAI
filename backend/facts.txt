# What is OpenLake
url: https://openlake.in
OpenLake is the open-source club of IIT Bhilai. It runs projects,
mentorship programmes and events around free and open-source software.

# Membership and fees
OpenLake is free to join. There is no membership fee of any kind.

## Who can join
url: https://openlake.in/join
Any current IIT Bhilai student can join, regardless of year or branch.
No prior open-source experience is needed.

## How to join
Come to any weekly meeting, or message the club on the community Discord.
There is no application form.
